MY PHOTO MY GAME: Business Idea

Tagline: Your Photos. Your Games. Your Challenge.

1. Concept

An Android gaming app where players upload 5–10 of their own photos and play puzzle and brain games using only those photos. Players compete on global, weekly, monthly and per-game leaderboards. Only username, avatar and score are public. Photos always stay private.

2. The problem and the hook
Most puzzle games use stock images, so they feel impersonal.
Playing with your own photos (family, friends, trips) is emotional, and that makes it shareable and habit-forming.
Leaderboards add competition on top of that.
3. Target audience

Teenagers, students, young adults, couples, friends, families and casual gamers. Bangladesh and South Asia are the first market, with global reach later.

4. Core product

Four games

Photo Puzzle (3×3, 4×4, 5×5, drag and drop)
Memory Match (6, 8 or 10 photo pairs)
Find the Difference (5 hidden differences)
Sliding Photo Puzzle (3×3 and 4×4)

Engagement features

Virtual points (completion, difficulty, time and perfect-play bonuses)
Levels and XP
Achievements and badges
Daily challenges
Login streaks
Game history and personal bests

Social features

Global, weekly, monthly and per-game leaderboards
Public player profiles (username, avatar, rank, best scores, badges)
Player search
5. Privacy as a selling point
Photos are stored in private storage and are never shown to other users.
Email, photos and game sessions are never public.
Users can delete their account and all their data.
6. Business model

Free plan (launch):

Registration
5 photos at first (a configurable maximum of 10)
All four games
All leaderboards

Future premium (tentative price: BDT 99–199 per month, not confirmed):

More photo storage
Premium themes
Advanced statistics
Exclusive achievements
Ad-free experience
Extra customization

Other possible revenue (to validate):

Light, non-intrusive ads on the free plan
Sponsored weekly tournaments
Cosmetic avatar and theme packs

Points are virtual only. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. This avoids gambling and legal issues.

7. Technology plan
App: Flutter and Dart (Android)
Backend: Java 21 with Spring Boot REST API
Database: Supabase PostgreSQL
Auth: Supabase Auth (email and Google)
Photo storage: Supabase Private Storage
Hosting: Render free tier, with GitHub for source control
Security: row-level security, server-side score validation, protected credentials, duplicate-submission protection, and account-deletion cleanup
8. Growth plan
Build the MVP, covering all four games, leaderboards and a photo library with a 5-photo minimum.
Run a closed beta with university and friend groups.
Launch on the Play Store, with a share-your-score feature.
Run weekly leaderboard events and a referral reward in virtual points.
Gather data on retention, the share of players who reach 5 photos, and games played per day. Introduce premium once there is a stable user base.
9. Key risks to check
Cheating: scores must be validated on the server.
Inappropriate photos: they are private, but you still need a policy and a report process.
Free hosting limits: Render's free tier sleeps when idle, and storage costs grow with users.
Retention: puzzle games are easily forgotten, so daily challenges and streaks are essential.
Privacy and law compliance: privacy policy, terms of service, and data deletion requests.

Database schema and API design
A privacy policy and terms of service draft
